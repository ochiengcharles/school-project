Welcome to the Admissions section of St. Peter's School. We are delighted that you are considering our school for your child's education. Our admission process is designed to be clear and straightforward, helping families understand what makes St. Peter's a unique place to learn and grow.

We seek students who are eager to learn, curious, and ready to contribute positively to our vibrant school community.

Our Admission Process

St. Peter's School admits students into various classes based on availability and successful completion of our admission requirements. We welcome applications for both day and boarding sections.

1

Inquiry & Information Gathering

Begin by exploring our website and, if possible, attend one of our open days or schedule a school tour to learn more about our programs and facilities.

Book a Tour
2

Application Submission

Download the application form from our website or collect it from the school office. Complete it thoroughly and submit it with all required documents.

Download Form
3

Entrance Examination / Interview

Prospective students will undergo an age-appropriate entrance examination or an interview to assess their academic readiness and personal attributes.

4

Acceptance & Enrollment

To accept the offer, parents must complete the enrollment agreement and pay the required school contribution by the specified deadline to secure their child's place if required.

*Please note that admission is subject to availability of space in the desired class and successful completion of all admission criteria.*

Admission Requirements

For S1 (Senior One)

  • Primary Leaving Examination (PLE) Certificate
  • Birth Certificate
  • Baptismal Card (for Catholic students)
  • Medical Form
  • Passport Photos (6 copies)

For S5 (Senior Five)

  • Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE)
  • Academic Transcripts
  • Letter of Transfer (if applicable)
  • Medical Form
  • Passport Photos (6 copies)
