Online Portal Login Guide

Use the correct portal for your role. The school must first create or approve your account; the portals do not provide shared default passwords.

Important: There are no default login credentials. Do not use another person's account, and never send passwords in a public group. If you do not have an account, contact the school administrator.

1. Teacher portal

Open Teacher Portal

  1. Ask the school administrator to create/approve your account in Supabase Authentication and assign your Auth user ID to your class and academic year.
  2. Use the exact email address registered by the administrator and the password assigned/set during account setup.
  3. Sign in, choose the student and reporting period, select the report-card file, and upload it.
  4. Tell the administrator when the report is ready for review and publication.

Email format: use your actual approved staff email, for example firstname.lastname@school-domain only if the school owns and configures that domain. No staff email accounts are automatically created by this website.

2. Parent/guardian portal

Open Parent/Guardian Portal

  1. Ask the school office to register your email and link your authenticated account to your child’s student record.
  2. Sign in with the same email and password registered for you.
  3. View report cards only after the school has published them and confirmed the guardian-to-student link.

Email format: use the parent's/guardian's own valid email address, such as their existing Gmail address. It does not need a special school-domain suffix. The admission number alone is not a login or password.

3. School administration dashboard

Open Administration Dashboard

  1. Only an authorised school administrator should use this portal.
  2. The first administrator account must be created by a trusted project owner in Supabase Authentication, then its user UUID must be added to the public.admin_users table.
  3. Sign in using that exact registered email and password. An ordinary teacher or parent account will not be granted admin access.
  4. Use the dashboard to add students, assign teachers, review applications/messages and publish report cards after checking them.

Email format: use the actual administrator account email chosen during account creation. A label such as admin@school-domain works only if that mailbox exists and the school owns the domain; it is not created automatically.

Can't sign in or page returns 404?

  • Open the portal links above from the live school website rather than an old bookmark.
  • Check the spelling of the URL and refresh after the latest deployment finishes.
  • If the page opens but login fails, confirm that your account exists in Supabase Authentication and that the email is verified if required.
  • For teachers, ask the administrator to confirm your class assignment. For parents, ask the office to confirm the guardian link.
  • Never share your password or use real student data while testing.

Portal access is controlled by Supabase authentication and database/storage policies. Creating a website link does not create an account.
